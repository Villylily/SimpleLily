# Simple Lily

Simple Lily (SL-MC) is a Multi Utilty Mincraft bot designed to do "Anything", survival gameplay, and combat. You may have seen it on my YouTube livestreams most likely not lol my channel is so dead.

## Features

### PvP
Yes sirrr we got this also best pvp bot???? (Note Simple Lily controls your client so you will need a second client to fight it server side realse mabye depends how many people love this)

### AFK
Keep your account active and managing tasks while you are away from your keyboard, Imagen being afk in a hardcore world but a baby zombie comes after you No worrys Lily will save you :p

### Oh No butttt Im lazzzyyy nooooo worrys Lilys here
Need to construct a Netherite beacon in a fresh world without lifting a finger? Simple Lily can handle gathering, crafting, and building for you completely automatically.
